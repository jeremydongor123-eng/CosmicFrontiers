COSMIC FRONTIER - 8 PAGE WEBSITE
Open index.html in a browser to start.

Pages:
1. index.html - Home
2. discover.html - Discover Space
3. stars.html - Stars and stellar evolution
4. missions.html - Space missions
5. news.html - Recent research and discoveries
6. future.html - Future missions and goals
7. assistant.html - Offline astronomy assistant and help centre
8. account.html - Login/create-account interface demonstration

All pages share style.css and app.js. Keep these files in the same folder.
The account page is NOT production authentication. It does not create accounts or save passwords.
The assistant is a built-in offline FAQ-style prototype, not a live AI service.
Mission dates and research summaries reflect sources checked on 24 November 2026 and may change.
